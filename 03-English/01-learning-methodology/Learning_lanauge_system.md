## Plan

### 英文會話必需的30句基本句型

#### **打招呼與介紹 (5句)**
1. Hello, how are you? / How's it going?
2. Nice to meet you. / It's nice meeting you.
3. What's your name? / My name is...
4. Where are you from? / I'm from...
5. How long have you been here?

#### **日常對話基礎 (8句)**
6. What do you do? / I work as...
7. How was your day? / It was good/fine.
8. What are you doing? / I'm just...
9. Do you like...? / I like/don't like...
10. What time is it? / It's... o'clock.
11. What's the weather like? / It's...
12. I agree. / I disagree.
13. That's interesting. / That sounds good.

#### **請求與回應 (6句)**
14. Could you help me? / Can you help me with...?
15. Would you mind if...? / Do you mind...?
16. I'd like to... / I want to...
17. Thank you very much. / Thanks a lot.
18. You're welcome. / No problem.
19. Excuse me. / Sorry about that.

#### **詢問信息 (6句)**
20. Where is...? / How do I get to...?
21. How much does this cost? / How much is it?
22. What does this mean? / I don't understand.
23. Could you repeat that? / Could you speak more slowly?
24. Do you speak English? / I don't speak... well.
25. What time does it start/end?

#### **表達意見與感受 (5句)**
26. I think... / In my opinion...
27. I feel... / I'm feeling...
28. That's a good idea. / That makes sense.
29. I'm sorry, but... / I'm afraid...
30. Let me think about it. / I'll get back to you.

### 學習原則

這些句型符合以下重要原則：

1. **實用性導向** - 這些都是日常會話中最常使用的句型
2. **反射性表達** - 通過反覆練習可以達到自然反應的程度
3. **涵蓋基本場景** - 包含了打招呼、請求、詢問、表達等核心交流需求

> 正如林語堂先生所說：「日常會話所應用的句極有限，日常需要的名詞、口頭話套總是說了又說，顛來倒去還是那些辭句。」
