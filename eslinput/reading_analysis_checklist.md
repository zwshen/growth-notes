# Reading Analysis Checklist for ESL Learners (Comprehensive Input Method)

This checklist provides a systematic approach to analyzing a text for comprehensive input, designed to maximize your English as a Second Language (ESL) learning.

## I. Principles of Comprehensive Input Analysis

This methodology focuses on deep engagement with the text to internalize vocabulary, grammar, and meaning, rather than just passive reading.

1.  **Active Engagement (主動參與)**: Don't just read; actively process each paragraph. (不僅僅是閱讀；要主動處理每個段落。)
2.  **Contextual Understanding (語境理解)**: Understand words and sentences within their specific context. (在特定語境中理解單詞和句子。)
3.  **Multilingual Reinforcement (多語言強化)**: Use your native language (Chinese) to clarify and solidify understanding. (使用你的母語（中文）來澄清和鞏固理解。)
4.  **Pronunciation Focus (發音重點)**: Integrate IPA to improve pronunciation alongside comprehension. (結合IPA來提高發音和理解。)
5.  **Iterative Learning (迭代學習)**: Apply this method consistently across different texts to build a strong foundation. (將此方法持續應用於不同的文本，以建立堅實的基礎。)

## II. Analysis Steps

### A. Pre-Reading (預讀)

*   [ ] **Skim the headings and subheadings (瀏覽標題和副標題):** Get a general idea of the topics covered in the chapter. (對章節涵蓋的主題有個大致了解。)
*   [ ] **Look at the images and captions (查看圖片和圖說):** They often provide important context. (它們通常提供重要的背景資訊。)
*   [ ] **Think about what you already know about the topic (思考你對該主題已知的內容):** Activate your prior knowledge. (激活你的先備知識。)

### B. During Reading (逐段分析)

For each paragraph:

*   [ ] **Read for the main idea (閱讀以掌握主旨):** What is the key message of this paragraph? Try to summarize it in your own words. (這個段落的關鍵訊息是什麼？嘗試用你自己的話總結它。)
    *   **Output Format (輸出格式):**
        ```
        **Paragraph Summary:** [English Summary]
        **段落大意:** [中文總結]
        ```

*   [ ] **Identify new or important vocabulary (識別生詞或重要詞彙):**
    *   [ ] Underline or highlight words you don't know or are unsure about. (劃線或標記你不認識或不確定的單詞。)
    *   [ ] Look up the definition in a dictionary. (查閱字典獲取定義。)
    *   [ ] Find the IPA transcription to practice pronunciation. (查找IPA音標以練習發音。)
    *   [ ] Write the word, its IPA, its English definition, and its Chinese interpretation. (寫下單詞、其IPA、英文定義和中文解釋。)
    *   **Output Format (輸出格式):**
        ```
        **Vocabulary:**
        *   **[Word]** (IPA: [IPA Transcription]): [English Definition]. ([中文解釋])
        ```

*   [ ] **Analyze sentence structure (分析句子結構):**
    *   [ ] Choose one or two interesting or complex sentences. (選擇一兩個有趣或複雜的句子。)
    *   [ ] Identify the subject, verb, and object. (識別主語、動詞和賓語。)
    *   [ ] Look for clauses, phrases, and connectors. (尋找子句、片語和連接詞。)
    *   [ ] Try to rewrite the sentence in a simpler way. (嘗試用更簡單的方式重寫句子。)
    *   **Output Format (輸出格式):**
        ```
        **Sentence Analysis:**
        *   "[Original Sentence]"
            *   [English Analysis of structure/meaning]
            *   **句子分析:** [中文分析]
        ```

*   [ ] **Engage with the content (與內容互動):**
    *   [ ] Ask yourself questions about the text (e.g., "Why did this happen?", "What is the evidence for this claim?"). (向自己提問關於文本的問題，例如：「為什麼會發生這種情況？」「這個主張的證據是什麼？」)
    *   [ ] Connect the information to your own experiences and knowledge. (將資訊與你自己的經驗和知識聯繫起來。)
    *   [ ] Take notes on the key points and your own thoughts. (記錄關鍵點和你的想法。)

### C. Post-Reading (閱讀後)

*   [ ] **Summarize the entire chapter (總結整個章節):** In your own words, explain the main arguments and conclusions of the chapter. (用你自己的話解釋章節的主要論點和結論。)
*   [ ] **Review your vocabulary list (複習你的詞彙表):** Practice the new words you have learned. (練習你學到的新單詞。)
*   [ ] **Discuss the chapter with someone (與他人討論章節內容):** Explaining the ideas to someone else is a great way to solidify your understanding. (向他人解釋想法是鞏固理解的好方法。)
*   [ ] **Write a short reflection (寫一篇簡短的反思):** What did you learn? What did you find most interesting? What questions do you still have? (你學到了什麼？你覺得什麼最有趣？你還有什麼問題？)
